The question

One of the central questions in psychiatry

The SEED Laboratory investigates why individuals with shared familial liability for psychosis and bipolar spectrum disorders diverge toward different developmental trajectories, with some progressing to serious mental illness while others remain resilient.

We seek to identify the earliest developmental paraphenotypes: traits that emerge in parallel with clinical symptoms from shared neurodevelopmental systems, and that underlie these distinct clinical outcomes. Our research focuses primarily on children and adolescents.

Key terms

A short glossary

Familial liability
Increased likelihood of a condition because a close relative, such as a parent or sibling, has it.
Paraphenotype
A measurable trait that emerges alongside clinical symptoms and arises from the same developing brain systems.
Divergence
The point where young people who share risk begin to follow different developmental paths.
